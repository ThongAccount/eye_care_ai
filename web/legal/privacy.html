01Who we are and what this covers

EyeCare AI ("the app") is published by TODO — legal entity / company name (TODO — registered address), a TODO — jurisdiction entity ("we", "us"). The app is a wellness and habit-tracking tool for the eyes. It is distributed as an Android application; the signed builds are published on the project's GitHub Releases page.

This policy covers the app running on your device. It does not cover the GitHub website, Google Play, or any other service you may use to download the app, each of which has its own privacy notice.

We have not built the app around behavioural advertising or third-party tracking. The app contains no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no crash-reporting SDK — its published dependency list is visible in the repository's pubspec.yaml.

02The short version

  • Most of what the app measures stays on your phone. Screen-time totals, eye-break counts, sleep estimates, outdoor minutes, ambient-light readings, eye-test results and camera measurements are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in. If you never create an account, no data is sent to our servers.
  • If you sign in, two things happen: your streak is published to the shared leaderboard, and your settings and habit history can be backed up to your private cloud backup document so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • The camera is opt-in and on-device. Frames are analysed by Google ML Kit on your phone to estimate viewing distance and whether your eyes are open. Images are never stored and never uploaded.
  • The AI assistant is the one feature that sends content off-device. Your chat messages, plus a short summary of your current settings, are sent to NVIDIA's API to generate a reply. Everything else stays local.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

04Data that stays on your device

The app writes almost everything it learns to the app's private shared preferences on your phone. That includes your screen-time totals, per-app usage breakdown, eye-break counts, daily score snapshots, factor percentages, survey answers, habit targets, outdoor and reading minutes, the eye timeline, eye-test history, and your reminder, theme, language and accessibility settings.

You can erase all of it from Settings → Privacy & security → Delete local data, and uninstalling the app removes it as well. TODO — confirm the exact menu labels in the current build before publishing

Camera frames are not stored

The eye test can optionally use the front camera to check whether your eyes are open and to estimate how far you are from the screen. Those frames are processed frame-by-frame in memory by Google ML Kit's on-device face model. The app does not write frames to disk and does not upload them; only the derived measurement (distance in centimetres, eye-open probability) is shown on screen, and that measurement is not persisted either.

Location is used as a signal, not a map

When outdoor tracking is enabled, the app asks for a GPS fix and uses only the answer to the question "could a GPS fix be obtained right now?" to accumulate outdoor minutes. For night-read mode it also stores your approximate latitude and longitude locally so it can compute local sunrise and sunset. Coordinates are never transmitted anywhere.

05Account and leaderboard data

If you create an account, it is an email/password or Google account handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The app receives your user ID, email address, display name and profile photo URL. It never sees or stores your password in plain text — password management and reset emails are handled by Google.

When your streak changes, the app writes one document to the Firestore collection leaderboard containing your display name, avatar URL, streak length, rank tier and a server timestamp. Other signed-in users can read the leaderboard, so treat the display name you choose as public. Guest use is possible for the local features; without an account nothing is published and nothing is backed up.

06Cloud backup and restore

Backup is optional and only runs for signed-in users. The app copies a fixed whitelist of local preference keys into a single private Firestore document named after your user ID. The whitelist deliberately excludes things that are not meaningful across a reinstall: running timers, in-progress notification state, eye-test history, eye-timeline events and camera-derived measurements.

The backup is uploaded in the background and restored automatically when you sign in on a new device. If you delete your account inside the app, the backup document is deleted too.

07The AI assistant

The assistant is optional and off until you open it and send a message. To produce a reply, the app sends your message text and the conversation so far to NVIDIA's integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1/chat/completions endpoint, together with a short context block describing your current settings (habit targets, vision profile, reminder style, and whether auto-reminders are on) so the assistant can give advice that fits your setup.

  • Messages are held in memory for the current app session; the app does not write the conversation to device storage.
  • The assistant may propose an action inside the app (for example, lowering a target, logging an eye break, turning on Focus Mode). By default the app asks you to confirm before anything is applied, and you can turn that confirmation off in Settings.
  • The assistant is advisory. It does not diagnose eye conditions and it never changes clinical settings.

12Your rights and controls

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. The app gives you direct control of most of these without contacting us:

  • Export — Settings → Export my data produces a JSON file of everything the app holds locally and hands it to the system share sheet.
  • Correct — edit targets, profile name and preferences directly in the app; changes are reflected in the next backup.
  • Delete local data — clears the app's local storage in one action.
  • Delete account — deletes the Firebase account and the cloud backup document, and clears local data.
  • Withdraw permissions — revoke camera, location or usage access in Android settings to stop a measurement.
  • Turn off data collection — the Data collection switch in Settings.

To exercise any right that the app does not cover, contact us at TODO — official privacy contact address. The in-app support mailbox currently used by the Help & Support section is eyecareai.app@gmail.com.
