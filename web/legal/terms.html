03What the scores and estimates mean

The Eye Health Score is a wellness estimate computed from your own device signals against the targets you set. It is not a clinical measurement and it is not calibrated against any medical standard. In particular:

  • Screen time is read from the Android operating system's own usage statistics, which can include time attributed to other apps and can be inaccurate on some devices.
  • Sleep is estimated from the gap between your last phone use before the night and your first use after waking. It is not a sleep measurement, it misses naps, and it counts time when you were awake but away from the phone.
  • Outdoor time is counted only when the device can obtain a GPS fix, so indoor time near a window may be over-counted and genuine outdoor time in poor signal may be under-counted.
  • Environment / dark-room comes from the ambient light sensor on your device, which is often less sensitive than a dedicated lux meter.
  • Viewing distance and eye openness are estimated on-device from camera frames using statistical models and population averages. The app itself documents an expected error of roughly ±15–20%.
  • The eye test is a short self-screening exercise based on your responses. It cannot detect refractive error, retinal disease, glaucoma, cataract or colour-vision deficiency.

A score that rises or falls tells you about your recent habits relative to your own targets. It says nothing about the condition of your eyes.

04The AI assistant

The assistant uses a third-party language model hosted by NVIDIA to produce replies. Its output is generated text, not professional advice, and it can be wrong, incomplete, or unsuitable for your situation. The assistant is instructed to refuse to diagnose and to suggest seeing an eye-care professional when symptoms are serious or persistent, but you should treat any output as unverified information.

The assistant can propose actions inside the app — for example, lowering a habit target, logging an eye break or switching Focus Mode on. Those actions are shown to you for confirmation before they run, and you can change that behaviour in Settings. You remain responsible for any change you accept.

When you use the assistant, your messages and a summary of your current settings are transmitted to NVIDIA. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
